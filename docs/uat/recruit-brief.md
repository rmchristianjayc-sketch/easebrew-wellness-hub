# Recruit Brief — Finding UAT Testers

**Goal:** 3–5 senior testers who mirror your real customer.

## Ideal tester profile

- **Age:** 50–75 (target customer range)
- **Tech comfort:** Uses Messenger daily. Owns a smartphone. Doesn't need to be tech-savvy.
- **Phone type:** at least 1 Android + 1 iPhone
- **Availability:** 15–20 minutes across 5 days (not one big session)
- **Willing to give honest feedback** — the point is to find problems, not to be polite

## Where to find them

1. **Immediate family** — parents, aunts, uncles, in-laws
2. **Church group members** — often the target demographic
3. **Existing satisfied customer** — pick 1–2 who already ordered EaseBrew and liked it
4. **Neighbor** — if 50+ and uses a phone

## What to avoid

- Do NOT recruit anyone under 40 as primary tester — they'll breeze through and miss the senior UX issues
- Do NOT recruit engineers or "power users" — they'll fixate on features, not usability
- Do NOT recruit anyone with a broken/old smartphone — you'll debug their phone, not the app

## Pitch script (Messenger, Tagalog)

> Kuya/Ate/Tita/Tito \[NAME\],
>
> Ilalabas na po namin ang bagong EaseBrew Wellness Hub — libreng app para sa mga customers namin. Bago i-release sa publiko, kailangan po namin ng ilang tester para tignan kung madali gamitin.
>
> 15–20 minutes lang po sa loob ng 5 days. Bibigyan ko po kayo ng libreng test code para masubukan lahat ng features.
>
> Willing po ba kayong tumulong? Salamat po!

## What you give each tester

1. Their unique test code (from `/admin/codes`)
2. The install link: `https://easebrew.com/verify` (or your production URL)
3. The Tagalog tester guide (from `tester-guide-tagalog.md`)
4. The checklist (from `tester-checklist.md`)

## What to promise (and NOT promise)

**OK to promise:**
- Free access to the app during testing
- Their feedback will directly shape the launch
- Personal thank-you (small gift optional — a pack of EaseBrew works)

**Do NOT promise:**
- Free lifetime access
- Payment for testing time
- That you'll fix everything they suggest (some are out of scope)
